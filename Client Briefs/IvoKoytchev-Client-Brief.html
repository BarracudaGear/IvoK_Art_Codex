Domain recommendation

Register ivokoytchev.art as the public address. It is your full name, it reads as an artist site, and it is available.

If you also want a traditional .com, add ivo-koytchev.com and we will point it to the same site. The unhyphenated name .com (ivokoytchev.com) is already taken by someone else and is only a parked page, not a studio site.
